# Products based on stVaults + DeFi Wrapper

:::info
If you're not sure which guide to follow to build your product, use our [**Guide Selector**](../index.mdx) to find the right one for your use case.
:::

1. [Conservative Multi-User Delegated Staking](./multi-user-delegated-staking.md) - An end-user staking product with a conservative, validation-based APR and a user-friendly interface that can be embedded into your own or a partner’s distribution channel.

2. [Multi-User Staking with Additional Yield via Custom DeFi Strategy](./multi-user-staking-with-custom-strategy.md) - An end-user staking product with a higher risk/yield profile achieved by depositing stETH minted from the stVault into a custom DeFi strategy, with a user-friendly interface that can be embedded into your own or a partner’s distribution channel.

3. [Multi-User Staking with Additional Yield via Lido EarnETH](./multi-user-staking-with-earn-eth.md) - An end-user staking product with a higher risk/yield profile achieved by depositing stETH minted from the stVault into the Lido EarnETH strategy, with a user-friendly interface that can be embedded into your own or a partner’s distribution channel.
