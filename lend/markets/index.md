# Markets

Each Lido Lend market lends one token against one collateral token and has its own parameters. A market's page lists its parameters and who can change them. The reasons for the parameters are in the market's post in the [Lido Lend thread](https://research.lido.fi/t/unveiling-lido-lend-security-first-lending/11987) on the Lido Research Forum.

## Ethereum mainnet

| Market                         | Loan token | Collateral | Liquidation LTV | Settlement | Status        | Forum post                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ---------- | ---------- | --------------- | ---------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [WETH / wstETH](./weth-wsteth) | WETH       | wstETH     | 98%             | Enabled    | Not activated | [WETH / wstETH market parameters](https://research.lido.fi/t/unveiling-lido-lend-security-first-lending/11987/78) |

The contract addresses of each market are on [Deployed contracts](../deployed-contracts).
