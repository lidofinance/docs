# Markets

Each Lido Lend market lends one token against one collateral token and has its own parameters. A market's page lists its parameters and who can change them, how its collateral is priced, and how freezes and pauses work in it.

## Ethereum mainnet

| Market                         | Loan token | Collateral | Liquidation LTV | Settlement | Status        |
| ------------------------------ | ---------- | ---------- | --------------- | ---------- | ------------- |
| [WETH / wstETH](./weth-wsteth) | WETH       | wstETH     | 98%             | Enabled    | Not activated |

The contract addresses of each market are on [Deployed contracts](../deployed-contracts).
